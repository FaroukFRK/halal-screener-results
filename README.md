## Method

1. **Business activity:** fail on conventional banking, insurance, alcohol, tobacco, gambling, cannabis (SEC industry code and company name); mixed industries (REITs, SPACs, hotels, restaurants, media, weapons, meat packing) go to REVIEW.
2. **Debt:** interest-bearing debt / market cap < 30%.
3. **Cash:** cash + interest-bearing securities / market cap < 30%.
4. **Income:** interest income / (revenue + interest income) < 5%.

Thresholds follow AAOIFI Shariah Standard No. 21. Market cap = last close × latest shares outstanding from SEC filings. Data: Nasdaq Trader symbol lists, Yahoo Finance prices, SEC EDGAR XBRL filings.
REVIEW means the screen could not decide (missing, stale or non-USD data, no revenue, or a mixed industry).

Updated automatically every weekday after the US market close by [a GitHub Action](.github/workflows/daily-screen.yml). Code: [`screener/`](screener/) · method details: [`docs/shariah-method.md`](docs/shariah-method.md).

> **Not investment advice and not a fatwa.** Automated data can be wrong or out of date. Penny stocks are high-risk. Verify each company yourself or with a qualified scholar.
